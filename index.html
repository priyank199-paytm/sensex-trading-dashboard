Protection Rules

• Up to 100 qualifying trades/day — not a trade quota.
• Hard daily loss lock: ₹8,000.
• Cooldown after 3 consecutive losses.
• Default maximum risk/trade: ₹4,000 and maximum capital deployed: ₹60,000.
• A-quality filter can reject marginal breakouts.
• Profit targets use configurable risk:reward instead of a forced monthly target.
No strategy can guarantee profit. Options can lose the entire premium rapidly. This dashboard remains simulation-only until a broker API is securely connected.
